# Credits and asset provenance

## Original project

Composition and project direction: **Ch3L1k**. Rendering, packaging and controls
were developed with AI coding assistance. The images are rendered by actual
ASCII glyphs in a GPU shader, rather than generated video or image-model frames.
Original code is MIT-licensed; this does not relicense third-party artwork.

## Painting — assets/figures.png

Adapted from **The Creation of Adam**, Michelangelo (1475–1564), c. 1508–1512.
The underlying painting is public-domain historical artwork. The supplied local
composition was made by Ch3L1k from a reproduction, cutting out the two figures
and placing them around Tux; the original PSD is not needed or distributed.
Reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Creaci%C3%B3n_de_Ad%C3%A1n.jpg
This reference identifies the painting; the exact source download URL of the
user-supplied reproduction was not recorded.

## Tux — assets/tux.png and docs/icon.png

**Larry Ewing** is the original Tux artist; original artwork made using **GIMP**.
Credit also to **Simon Budig** (vector version), **Anja Gerwinski** (drawing
improvements) and **Garrett LeSage** (later SVG work, CC0). The supplied image was a raster rendition named Tux.svg.webp,
extracted from the user's composition and resized with no design changes.
Reference and artwork permissions: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tux.svg
The original redistribution notice is retained in LICENSES/Tux-README.txt.
Original attribution is retained. Tux is not claimed as original project artwork
or as artwork owned by the Linux Foundation.

## Glyph atlas — assets/glyphs.png

Rasterized from **DejaVu Sans Mono**, descended from Bitstream Vera.
The font file is not shipped. Its notice is retained in LICENSES/DejaVu.txt.
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

## Wayland layer-shell protocol

protocol/wlr-layer-shell-unstable-v1.xml contains Drew DeVault's original
copyright and permissive license. The notice is preserved in the file.
The xdg-shell protocol is generated at build time from the system-installed
wayland-protocols package; that package's notices apply to its generated code.
